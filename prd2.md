Who plays: One player.
On screen: You steer a pizza left and right with ← and → as it moves down a conveyor belt. Catch the toppings shown on the order card and dodge everything else.
Scoring: Each correctly finished pizza earns 1 point. A mistake ruins that pizza, then the next order starts. The conveyor gradually speeds up as your score grows.
How it ends: The game keeps going until you ruin 3 pizzas. Your final score appears beside your highest score, with a Play Again button. Your highest score is saved for next time.
